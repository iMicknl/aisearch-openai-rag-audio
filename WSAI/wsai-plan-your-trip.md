### Travel Planner

9292 [https://www.9292.nl/en) is the public travel planner site for the
area for trains, buses, metro, trams and boats. It can be
used to view bus times from Zaandam to the Taets area.
But note, it is an 11 minute walk from the nearest bus stop.


## Buses

Taets Art & Event Park is near Zaandam train station.
Several other buses drive between the station and the
Hembrugterrein (https://www.taets.com/en/hembrug-terrain/). For Taets take the
smaller 'buurtbus' 312 which will take you to the Hemkade
entrance of the terrain, and the entrance of Taets, in less
than 10 minutes.

Please enter the address into your advice for directions
from your location, or click the Google Maps link to the

Arriving by Public Transport

Zaandam train station is 2.8km from the Taets Art & Event
Park, which is approximately a 35 minute walk. There are
frequent taxi services available from Zaandam train
station, and Public Transport options. Click here
[https://www.taets.com/en/route/) for more information directly from
the Taets Art & Event Park website.

## Bicycle [OV-fiets]

At the train station, you can rent a public transport bicycle.
This will take you to the terrain in less than 10 minutes. For
bicycle storage, follow the city centre exit and take the
escalator down and turn left .- From the bicycle storage,
cross the provincial road and turn left behind the station
\\- After approximately 200m, you will cross under the road,
after which you turn left. Follow the cycling path to the
right and continue on provincial road until you reach the
water. Turn left and after approximately 50 metres you will
see the entrance to the Hembrugterrein to your left. Inside
the terrain, the Taets Art & Event Park is on your right.
